Boston’s gay and inclusive rugby club brings its Come & Scrum fundraiser to Halloween afternoon, this time with a 1920s “All That Jazz” theme at the Harbor Lounge on the waterfront. It’s a daytime hang with the team between brunch and tea, and it lines up nicely with Saturday night’s Roaring Twenties costume ball at the Crown if you’re planning one look for the day.

Past editions during Bear Week have run with no cover, raffle prizes and drink specials, with proceeds supporting the club, a 501(c)(3). The flyer doesn’t list a price or ticket link for this one, so expect the same format and bring cash for raffles.
